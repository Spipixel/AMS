Academy Management System:
	1. Course/Scheduling module:
		i. Add, View, update, and archive courses
		ii. Set fees, capacity, duration, and regular schedule
		iii. Assign  a primary teacher and a replacement teacher
		iv. Detect Scheduling conflicts
		v. Cancel or reschedule student class session
	2. Teacher Module:
		i. Register, and view all teachers
		ii. Track their specialities and assigned course	
		iii. Display each/individual teacher(s) schedule
		iv. Track/summarise each teacher attendance (summarise means get the % of teacher attendance attending daily scheduled class sessions)
		v. prevent overlapping teaching assignments
	3. Student and enrollment module:
		i. Register students and validate their details	
		ii. Enroll and unenroll students
		iii. Reject invalid course IDs and duplicate active enrollments
		iv Prevent enrollment beyond course capacity
		v. Show each student's active courses
	4. Finance Module:
		i. Student fee entry
		ii. Billed Amount (if partial payment is made, if payment is fully paid, or overpaid)
		iii. Teacher salary entry
		iv. Display all pending and completed payment in filtered format
		v. Keep payment history instead of overwiriting a paid or unpaid flag
	5. Attendance and registration module
		i. Student attendance entry for a specific session
		ii. Teacher attendance entry for a specific session
		iii. Prevent duplicated attendance entires
		iv. generate attendance percentage and absence report
	6. Report Module:
		i. Show running and canceled classes
		ii. Display total students, teachers, and active courses
		iii. Show available time slots and emprty time time slots
		iv. Generate daily attendance and financial summaries
		v. Display notices about canceled or scheduled change
	


Situation											Expected behavior
Invalid course ID during enrollment									Reject enrollment with a clear message
Course has no available seats										Reject enrollment
Two courses need the same room at the same time								Reject the conflicting schedule
Teacher already has a class at that time								Reject the conflicting assignment
Primary teacher is absent										Assign an eligible replacement or mark the session canceled
Course is removed											Preserve historical records; don't silently erase them
Student pays part of their fee										Record the payment and calculate the remaining balance
Teacher receives part of their salary									Record the payment and retain the outstanding balance
Attendance is entered twice for the same student and session						Prevent the duplicate
A class is canceled											Exclude it from normal completed-class attendance calculations
