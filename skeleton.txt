Module	Main responsibility
0. Storage	                Read, write, append, update, and validate stored records
1. Shared utilities	        IDs, validation, date/time handling, errors
2. Course management	    Course lifecycle, fees, capacity, schedules
3. Scheduling and sessions	Time-slot availability, conflicts, cancellations, replacements
4. Teacher management	    Registration, specialties, schedules, assignments
5. Student and enrollment	Registration, enrollment, capacity and duplicate checks
6. Finance	Student fees,   payment history, teacher payroll
7. Attendance	            Student and teacher attendance, duplicate prevention
8. Reports and notices	    Dashboard, attendance, finance, and schedule reports
9. CLI	                    Menus, user input, display, application entry point
